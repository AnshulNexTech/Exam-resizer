Avoid Form Rejection: 4 Quick Checks Before Uploading

  • Plain White Background: Stand against a light/white wall with even lighting and no shadows on your face.
  • Black Ink Signature: Sign on clean white unruled paper using a dark black pen, then crop tightly around the signature.
  • No Blur or Pixelation: If the file size is too small, slightly increase the quality slider above while staying within the KB range.
  • Exact Filename: For UPSC forms, do not rename the downloaded photo.jpg or signature.jpg files.
